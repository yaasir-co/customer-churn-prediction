# Module 03 — Exploratory Data Analysis

## Why EDA matters
EDA is used to understand the dataset before choosing models.

## Investigate
- Class distribution
- Missing values
- Numeric distributions
- Category frequencies
- Churn rate by segment
- Outliers
- Unexpected relationships
- Potential leakage

## Business examples
Compare churn rates for:
- Month-to-month vs long-term contracts
- New vs long-tenure customers
- Fiber vs DSL
- Electronic check vs automatic payment
- Customers with vs without technical support

## Important caution
EDA reveals associations, not proof of causation.
